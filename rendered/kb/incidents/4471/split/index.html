#4471 is closed, but not every report in it is solved. The one-day moves were never part of the parser fix. Keep their ticket open until someone owns the time-zone bug.

The problems

parser · day and month flipped. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. Every channel, every tenant. Fixed 5 October (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales).

hint · the booking-v3 form asks for MM/DD. Under its date field, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant's way, day first. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it. Won't fix: booking-v3 is retired, so the bug leaves with the form.

one-day · the date moves by a day. These reports do not flip day and month: Tuesday shows as Monday, a meeting lands on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, every booking in June is a day off, an invite arrives before the booking. That points at time zones or the summer-time change, not at date order. The desk had already set them apart in desk-notes.json, and then filed them under parser by tenant. Still open: the parser fix does not move a date by a day.

Why one ticket hid them

  • We merged on the subject line (decision 0001). "Wrong date" reads the same whatever broke it. The cause depends on the tenant and the way the report came in, and the subject says neither.
  • The intake tag is a guess from the reporter's browser, not from the tenant. Counting by tag gives wrong totals both ways; the column above shows how many each problem would lose.
  • The export repeats rows where its pages overlap. Count by report id, once.
